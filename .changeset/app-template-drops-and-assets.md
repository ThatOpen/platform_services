The app template drops files on the project as the Hub does: what is dropped on the app is uploaded to the project, converted on the server and opened (`<top-documents-panel reads-project opens-drops>`). Someone who may not upload is told so. While it works, the status chip tells uploading from processing, the documents list shows a progress line with the time left, and a file can be cancelled as it is processed. Assets, the project's documents, comes first and is opened by default.
