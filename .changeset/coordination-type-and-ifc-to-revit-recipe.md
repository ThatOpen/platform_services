Documents: a fifth `DocumentType`, `'coordination'` ("Coordination set": a project's topics, kept as a BCF). `.bcf` and `.bcfzip` files read as coordination, and `list({ types: ['coordination'] })` lists the Coordinator's sets. The IFC→Revit recipe also improves: one prism family per profile however it is turned, structural columns where the file says so, shapes placed on their side share a family by their turn about Z, and a sloped footprint roof stands on its bottom at the eaves.
